# Privacy Policy

**CIGA Interaction Studio (C.I.G.A.I.S.)**

Last updated: September 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

CIGA Interaction Studio is a locally operated desktop application designed for VTuber interaction, streaming, event handling, and external output workflows.

The application is designed to keep its core functionality on the user's own computer. CIGA does not require a central account or cloud service for its core local functionality.

## 2. Information Stored Locally

CIGA may store information locally on the user's computer, including:

- Application settings and configuration
- CIGA profiles
- Imported asset references and local asset data
- StreamElements credentials configured by the user
- VTube Studio authentication tokens
- Other authentication or session information required by integrations explicitly enabled by the user

These files are stored locally and are not intended to be publicly shared.

Users are responsible for protecting local profile and configuration files that may contain authentication or session credentials.

## 3. External Services and Network Connections

CIGA may communicate with external services when the user explicitly configures or enables an integration.

Depending on the features used, these services may include:

- StreamElements
- VTube Studio
- CIGAIS services
- Discord-connected CIGA event services

These connections are used to provide functionality requested or enabled by the user.

CIGA does not require external network communication for its core local functionality.

## 4. StreamElements

When StreamElements integration is enabled, CIGA may use credentials or authorization information configured by the user to communicate with StreamElements services.

The credentials required for this integration are stored locally by CIGA.

CIGA does not intentionally transmit StreamElements credentials to unrelated third parties.

StreamElements remains responsible for its own services and privacy practices.

## 5. VTube Studio

When VTube Studio integration is enabled, CIGA communicates with the VTube Studio API according to the configuration provided by the user.

Authentication information required for this integration is stored locally by CIGA.

VTube Studio remains responsible for its own services and privacy practices.

## 6. CIGAIS and Live Image Events

Some CIGA features may use CIGAIS services to process event data and deliver configured content to the application.

For live image events, the CIGAIS system may receive and process information required to deliver the event, including image URLs and related event metadata.

Depending on the configured event workflow, CIGA may download an image from a URL supplied through the CIGAIS service and display it locally in the CIGA viewport.

Users should only enable live image functionality in communities and environments where they trust the participants.

## 7. Twitch and Discord Identity Matching

CIGA's current live image event system does not guarantee that the Discord user submitting an image is the same person who triggered the corresponding Twitch event.

Twitch and Discord identities are not currently linked by the CIGA system.

As a result, another participant may submit the image that is processed for an active event.

## 8. Analytics and Tracking

CIGA does not intentionally use analytics or advertising tracking as part of its core local functionality.

The application is designed to operate locally without requiring a centralized telemetry system.

Third-party services connected by the user may have their own analytics, logging, or tracking practices. Those practices are governed by the respective third-party service providers.

## 9. Data Transmission

CIGA does not intentionally transmit user information to external services unless a feature or integration has been explicitly configured or enabled by the user.

When an external integration is enabled, information may be transmitted to the corresponding service as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

CIGA cannot control how third-party services process information after transmission. Users should review the privacy policies of any external services they choose to connect to CIGA.

## 10. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Protecting locally stored authentication tokens and credentials
- Avoiding the public sharing of CIGA profile files containing sensitive information
- Reviewing the privacy practices of external services they connect to CIGA
- Configuring integrations according to their own privacy and community requirements
- Ensuring that content submitted through live event features is appropriate for their intended environment.

## 11. Children's Privacy

CIGA is a general-purpose desktop application and does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a centralized CIGA account or tracking system.

Any information processed through third-party integrations is subject to the policies and requirements of those third-party services.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CIGA's functionality, integrations, or data handling practices change.

Updated versions will be published in the public CIGA Interaction Studio repository.

## 13. Contact

Questions, concerns, or privacy-related reports can be submitted through the public CIGA Interaction Studio GitHub repository:

https://github.com/gabobars/CIGA-Interaction-Studio

Users may open an issue in the repository for privacy-related questions or concerns.
